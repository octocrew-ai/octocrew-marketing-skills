# 📊 Funnel Quality Review

Find where customers drop out of your funnel and which traffic sources produce qualified leads, purchases, or paid customers. Use it when conversion rates drop or cheap leads rarely turn into sales.

## What to bring

- Counts for the funnel stages you track, such as visits, form submissions, qualified leads, and sales.
- One reporting period and the split you want to compare, such as campaign, landing page, or device.
- Spend by segment when you want to compare acquisition costs.

## What you get

- Conversion rates between stages and the biggest drop-off by segment.
- Cost per first conversion alongside cost per qualified lead or customer when the data supports it.
- A likely cause to investigate, one way to check it, and a proposed change for approval.

## Try it

> Compare our paid search and paid social campaigns for the past month. Show each step from first visit to paying customer and tell me where the biggest difference appears.

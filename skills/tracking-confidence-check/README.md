# 🎯 Tracking Confidence Check

Check whether your conversion data is reliable enough to guide a paid media decision. Use this before changing spend when results look unusually good, come in late, or disagree across systems.

## What to bring

- The reporting period, ad spend, conversion counts, and the platform's attribution window.
- A second source when available, such as your store, CRM, backend, or GA4.
- Any recent changes to tags, checkout, consent, or conversion events.

## What you get

- A high, medium, or low confidence rating based on the evidence.
- A recommendation to act, act cautiously, or wait and investigate.
- Specific checks for delays, duplicates, mismatched attribution windows, and gaps in source data.

## Try it

> Meta reports 44 purchases and Shopify shows 29 for the same week. Check whether we can use this week's ROAS to make a budget decision and tell me what to verify first.

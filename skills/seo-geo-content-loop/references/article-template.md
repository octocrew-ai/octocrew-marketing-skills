---
title: <Question the article answers, in the reader's words>

slug: <short-keyword-slug>
target_query: <main query>
related_queries: <2–5 queries>
status: draft            # draft → in review → approved → published
---

## Checklist before review

- [ ] Opening answers the question.
- [ ] Key takeaways and FAQ present.
- [ ] Every external fact has a primary-source URL in notes.
- [ ] Company claims match approved facts.
- [ ] No invented numbers, cases or guarantees.
- [ ] Title, meta description, slug set.
- [ ] Internal links added.

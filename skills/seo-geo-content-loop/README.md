# 🔎 SEO + GEO Content Loop

Run a weekly SEO review, choose which pages to create or refresh, and check where your brand appears in AI answers. The skill turns Search Console and GA4 data into a plan a marketer can review and approve.

## What to bring

- A site URL and read access to Google Search Console and GA4, or comparable exports.
- Your audience, markets, languages, priority topics, and approved company facts.
- The person who reviews content and approves publishing or site changes.

## What you get

- A weekly comparison of search performance and content opportunities backed by query evidence.
- A technical check with affected URLs and proposed fixes.
- A draft and fact-checking workflow, plus a log of sample questions, brand mentions, and citations in AI answers.

AI visibility checks capture a sample of answers for specific prompts and dates.

## Try it

> Run our weekly SEO report for the last seven complete days. Compare it with the previous seven days and recommend three pages to refresh, with the query evidence for each.
